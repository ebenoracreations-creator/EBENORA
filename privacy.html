Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ebenora Creations ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit our website, contact us, or chat with a WhatsApp business assistant that we operate for ourselves or on behalf of our clients (for example, solar installation businesses).

In short: we only collect the details you choose to share with us, we use them to answer your enquiry and contact you about it, and we never sell your personal information.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us, for example when you message us on WhatsApp, fill a form, or email us:

  • Your name and WhatsApp/phone number (and your WhatsApp profile name)
  • Your area, pincode or a location you choose to share
  • Details about your enquiry, such as property type (home, shop, factory), approximate monthly electricity bill, whether you own the property, and whether you want a site visit or a call
  • Any questions or messages you send us

Information collected automatically:

  • If you reach us through an advertisement (for example a Click-to-WhatsApp ad on Facebook or Instagram), Meta may share basic ad details with us, such as the ad headline or ad ID you clicked
  • Technical information such as message timestamps and delivery status
  • Standard website analytics (pages visited, device and browser type) if you use our website

2. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and provide the information you asked for
  • To arrange a call, quote or site visit when you request one
  • To pass your enquiry to the business you contacted (for example, the solar company whose ad you responded to) so they can serve you
  • To answer common questions automatically through our chat assistant
  • To improve our services and measure how well our advertising works
  • To meet legal, tax or regulatory obligations

3. Automated replies and AI

Our WhatsApp assistant sends automatic replies and asks a few questions to understand your requirement. If you type a free-form question, the text of that question may be processed by an AI service (such as Google Gemini) to generate a helpful reply. We do not send your name or phone number to the AI service for this purpose. A member of our team can always take over the conversation; just ask.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with:

  • The business you enquired with, when we operate a chat service on its behalf
  • Service providers that help us run our services, including Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform), Google (Google Workspace / Google Sheets for storing enquiries, Gmail for notifications, and Gemini for AI replies) and Cloudflare (secure hosting of our chat system). These providers process data under their own security and privacy terms.
  • Authorities, where we are required to by law

5. How long we keep it

Temporary chat progress is deleted automatically after 7 days of inactivity. Enquiry records are kept for as long as needed to serve you and follow up on your enquiry, and generally no longer than 24 months, unless a longer period is required by law. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

6. How we protect it

We use reputable cloud providers, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls so that only authorised people can see enquiry records. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Stop messages: simply reply "STOP" or block the number on WhatsApp at any time.
  • Access, correct or delete: contact us and we will help you see, correct or delete the information we hold about you.
  • Withdraw consent: you may withdraw consent to our use of your information at any time. This will not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

We process personal data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and respect equivalent rights for users in other countries.

8. Children

Our services are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.
